Leading the call
“You may not have a vote in this hall, but you have a voice.”
Hon. Mia Amor MottleyPrime Minister of Barbados
Chair, Climate Vulnerable Forum and V20

At COP31, Mia Amor Mottley, Prime Minister of Barbados and Chair of the Climate Vulnerable Forum and V20, challenged the world to recognise that the climate crisis is too important to be left only to those at the negotiating table. Not everyone has a seat at that table, but everyone has a stake in what happens next.

Our goal is to bring together 1.5 billion voices before COP32, united in the call to keep the 1.5°C goal alive and demand stronger climate action. By joining, you will add your voice to a global call for governments to cut emissions faster, for financial institutions to make climate solutions affordable, and for leaders to demonstrate what they have delivered.

Together, we will use this public support to press for concrete commitments and measurable progress before, during and beyond COP32. Every voice strengthens the demand. Every commitment must lead to action.

Why now

The world is getting warmer.
Our voices must get louder.

We see it in stronger storms, vanishing coastlines, failed crops and communities rebuilding again and again. We have the science and many of the solutions. What we need now is the collective will to act.

2.8°C

Where current policies are taking the world, according to the United Nations. The goal is 1.5°C.

Every fraction of a degree matters. For some, it means hotter summers. For others, it is the difference between keeping a home and losing it.

What we are calling for

We are not asking for another conversation.
We are asking the world to act.

Greater ambition

Stronger climate commitments from every government, backed by credible action.

Fairer finance

A financial system that makes climate solutions affordable, especially for vulnerable countries.

Real accountability

Measurable progress, not promises that disappear between climate conferences.

Signing is just the beginning

Turn your voice into action.

01

Speak up.

Ask your elected representatives what they are doing to protect the 1.5°C goal. Make it part of the public conversation.

Dear [Representative], I have joined the global call for 1.5 billion voices for 1.5 degrees. Where does our Parliament stand on keeping global warming to 1.5°C, and what will our country commit to before COP32? I would like your answer on the record.
02

Follow the money.

Ask your bank and pension fund how your money is shaping the planet's future, and push for investments that build a safer world.

To my bank / pension fund: I'm a customer and I've joined the call for 1.5 billion voices for 1.5 degrees. Please tell me how my money is invested, what share goes to fossil fuels, and what share goes to clean energy and climate resilience in vulnerable countries.
03

Grow the movement.

Invite two people: family, friends, colleagues. Movements do not begin with billions. They begin with people who refuse to stay silent.
