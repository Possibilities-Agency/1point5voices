Privacy notice

How we look after your information

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to count your voice: your first name, email, country, how you are joining and an optional message.
  • Only your first name, country and message appear publicly, and only if you choose.
  • Your email is never shown publicly, sold or shared with other organisations for their own use.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

1.5 Billion Voices for 1.5 Degrees (“the campaign”) is a global movement answering the call made at COP31 on behalf of the V20 countries. The campaign website is owned and operated by The Possibilities Agency (TPA), Barbados, on behalf of the Government of Barbados.

TPA is the data controller for the personal data collected through this website under the Data Protection Act, 2019-29 of Barbados (“the Act”).

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
First nameTo count your signature and, if you choose, show it on the public wall.
Email addressTo make sure each person is counted once, to contact you about your signature, and to send updates if you opt in.
CountryTo show where voices are coming from on the map and in campaign totals.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data, such as health, religion or political affiliation. Please do not include it in your message.

Technical information. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the website securely and to block automated or fraudulent sign-ups. We do not store your IP address with your signature. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a small note that you have signed so the form is not shown to you again; this stays on your device.

3. Our legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign. Sending you campaign updates is a separate consent, given only if you tick the updates box. You can withdraw either consent at any time (see section 9). Withdrawing does not affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew.

4. What is public

  • Always public, as totals only: the number of signatures, the number by country and the number by type of participant. No one can be identified from these totals.
  • Public only if you tick the box: your first name, country, how you are joining and your message, on the website's wall and in the live feed.
  • Never public: your email address and whether you opted in to updates.

5. Who we share your information with

We never sell your personal data.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website and stores the signature database as our data processor, acting only on our instructions.
  • The Government of Barbados, V20 partners and other campaign partners receive campaign totals and, where relevant, public messages. We do not give them signers' email addresses.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires us to.

6. International transfers

This is a global campaign, and our hosting provider stores and processes data on servers that may be located outside Barbados. When you sign, you consent to your personal data being transferred outside Barbados for the purposes in this notice. We rely on our provider's contractual commitments and security safeguards to protect it wherever it is processed.

7. How long we keep it

  • Signatures: kept for the life of the campaign and deleted, or reduced to anonymous totals, within [six months] after the close of COP32.
  • Updates list: kept until you unsubscribe or the campaign ends, whichever comes first.

8. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit, the database is not publicly accessible, and access is limited to authorised TPA staff who need it for the campaign. If a personal data breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the Data Protection Commissioner and, where required, you, as the Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it: have inaccurate or incomplete data fixed.
  • Erase it: ask us to delete your signature and data.
  • Restrict it: ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Take it with you: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Object and withdraw consent: stop updates or withdraw your signature at any time.
  • Complain: to the Data Protection Commissioner (section 11).

To use any of these rights, email [privacy@yourdomain] from the address you signed with. Every update email will also include an unsubscribe link. There is no charge, and we will respond without undue delay and within the time the Act allows.

10. Young people

Young people's voices matter to this movement. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign, and leave the public-wall box unticked if you would rather not appear publicly.
